What makes a slot "advantage play"?

Most slots are negative expectancy if you just mash spin. Advantage play slots are different: the game state, jackpot level, or promotion window can change the EV enough that the correct decision is to play... or to walk. Players who track those conditions are hunting edge, not chasing random hits.

Common AP families include persistent-state titles, Must Hit By progressives, linked bank games, and other machines where take points and floor reads matter more than luck folklore.
