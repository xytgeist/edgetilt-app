EdgeTilt SMS — verbal opt-in script

EdgeTilt.com · transactional poker tournament swap notifications · effective 2026-07-31

Service

One-way transactional SMS for poker tournament swap invitations and result updates on edgetilt.com. No marketing or promotional messages.

How consent is collected

Before an EdgeTilt member enters a counterparty’s mobile number, the member obtains verbal consent in person or by phone. The counterparty must agree and provide their number before any SMS is sent.

Verbal script (member to counterparty)

“EdgeTilt can send you text messages about our tournament swap — the swap offer, a secure link to accept or enter your result, and updates when results are logged. You’ll typically get one to three messages per swap, only while that swap is active. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help or STOP to opt out at any time. Our Terms of Service are at https://edgetilt.com/terms and our Privacy Policy is at https://edgetilt.com/privacy. Is it OK if I enter your mobile number in EdgeTilt to send you those swap texts? Please say yes or no.”

After consent

  • If the counterparty says yes and provides their number, the member enters it in EdgeTilt Poker Bankroll (guest swap form) and sends the swap.
  • The first transactional SMS confirms enrollment for that swap (offer + secure claim link).
  • If the counterparty says no, no number is entered and no SMS is sent.

Message frequency

Approximately one to three messages per active swap (offer, optional result/settlement updates).

Opt-out and help

Recipients may reply STOP to opt out or HELP for assistance.
