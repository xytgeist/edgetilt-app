EdgeTilt Fantasy is for leagues you already play. It's not another Sleeper, Yahoo, ESPN, or CBS, and it's not an expert rankings list.

Connect

  • Sleeper uses your username. No password.
  • Yahoo uses a sign-in you approve. Your leagues won't load until Yahoo approves the app's access.
  • ESPN and CBS use a login sheet in the app, where you sign in on that host. EdgeTilt doesn't ask you to type that password into a form we store.

ESPN and CBS only connect from the app, not the browser.

What you get

Once a league connects, you get four tools: Roster, Live, Waivers, and Trades.

Roster is start/sit from your own players, in that league's scoring. Live is the current week. Waivers ranks the adds available in that league. Trades suggests deals built from your roster and theirs. You still make claims, bids, and lineup changes on the host. EdgeTilt doesn't file them.

The league shown above is a demo with made-up teams. Your real leagues, rosters, and scores stay in your account.

FAQ

Which hosts can I connect?

Sleeper, Yahoo, ESPN, and CBS. Sleeper takes a username, Yahoo a sign-in you approve, and ESPN and CBS a login sheet in the app.

Do you store my ESPN or CBS password?

No. You sign in on that host's sheet. EdgeTilt doesn't ask you to type that password into a form we store.

Will EdgeTilt set my lineup or submit a waiver?

No. It shows the move. You make it on the host.

Is this a rankings site?

No. There's no expert board and no DFS lobby. The tools read the leagues you connect.

Does this page show my league?

No. What you see here is a demo league with made-up teams. Create an account and connect your host to see your own league.
