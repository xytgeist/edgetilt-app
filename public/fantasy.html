EdgeTilt Fantasy is for people who already play season-long fantasy and want the week's calls tied to their own roster. It's a command center for your leagues on Sleeper, Yahoo, ESPN, or CBS. It's not a copy of those sites, and it's not a board of expert rankings you could read without a team.

What matters is the roster that's actually yours. Who's in your lineup, who's sitting on waivers in that league, and what a trade does to your team. A generic top-50 list doesn't know your byes, your bench, or your scoring.

Fantasy is an account tool, and this page is only the intro. It won't load a league, a score, a waiver wire, or a lineup. Not yours, not anybody's.

What you get in your account

Once you join, you connect a host you already play on. Sleeper connects with your username and only sees leagues already on that Sleeper account. Yahoo connects through a sign-in you approve. ESPN and CBS connect through a login sheet in the EdgeTilt app, where you sign in on that host, captcha and all. EdgeTilt doesn't ask you to type that host's password into a form we store.

A connected league can show your teams and this week's matchup, plus roster tools that start from your players. That means help with who to start, who to look at on waivers, and how a trade changes your roster. The advice is about your roster. It's not a published rank list.

How it works

  1. Create an account and open Fantasy.
  2. Pick the host you already use.
  3. Approve the connection. On Sleeper, that's your username. On Yahoo, it's a sign-in you approve. On ESPN and CBS, it's the in-app login sheet.
  4. EdgeTilt reads the leagues that host already has for you. A Sleeper username won't pull in an ESPN league, and pasting a public league ID doesn't count as connecting.
  5. Work your matchup from your own players. League data stays in your account.

What this page will not do

It won't show a player, a projection, a score, or a lineup. It won't rank the waiver wire for the public. It won't log you into a host on its own. Daily contest sites are a different mode, and this intro doesn't load them.

FAQ

Does this page show my league?

No. Leagues, matchups, and lineups open in your account after you connect a host. None of them show up on this page.

Do you store my ESPN, Yahoo, or CBS password?

No. Sleeper uses your username. Yahoo uses a sign-in you approve. ESPN and CBS use a login sheet in the app where you sign in on that host. EdgeTilt doesn't ask you to type that password into a form we store.

Is this a list of expert rankings?

No. The tools work off the roster you connected. A public rank list isn't the product.

Can I use Fantasy without an account?

No. Connecting a host and opening a league both happen in your account. This page doesn't load either one.

Is Fantasy the same thing as Sports Bet Tracker?

No. The bet tracker is a log of bets you enter. Fantasy is for leagues you already play. The public calculators are their own pages, and they don't read your leagues or your bets.

Will I see other people's lineups here?

No. There's no league data on this page. What you connect stays with your account.
