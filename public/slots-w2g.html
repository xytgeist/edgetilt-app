The W-2G Scanner is for anyone who got a tax slip from a casino and wants the boxes read into a private archive for the year. A W-2G is the form a casino hands you for certain gambling winnings. The scanner reads a form you give it and stores it with your EdgeTilt account. It doesn't prepare a return, tell you what you owe, or publish jackpots.

This page is just the intro. It doesn't show a slip, a name, a taxpayer number, or an amount. There's no sample filled out with someone else's form, either.

What you get in your account

Once you join, you open W-2G Scanner from the slots tools. Snap a photo of a W-2G you got, or pick one you already took. The app reads the form and shows you the fields before anything saves. Fix any box that doesn't match the paper, then save. The image and the fields go into an archive that belongs to your account, and you can remove a slip from it later.

If you log a jackpot cash-out in the play logbook, EdgeTilt can offer to scan the W-2G and fill in the date and amount from that entry. You still check the paper. The logbook doesn't replace the slip.

Reading and saving one slip at a time is free with an account. Importing a stack of slips in one pass is Slots Edge and up. Nothing gets imported from this page.

How it works

  1. Create an account and open W-2G Scanner.
  2. Take a photo of your own form in the app, or choose one you already have.
  3. Look over the boxes the scanner filled in. Change any field that doesn't match the paper.
  4. Save. The slip is stored on your account, not on this public page.
  5. When you file, go by the paper form and your own records. The scanner reads and stores slips. It's not tax advice.

What this page will not do

It won't show an archive. It won't guess a filing status, a deduction, or a refund. It won't show a casino's copies of other people's W-2Gs. The only slips in an archive are the ones that account added.

FAQ

Is this tax advice?

No. It reads and stores a W-2G you give it. You file from the slip and your own records. EdgeTilt doesn't file a return for you and doesn't tell you what you owe.

Can I try a scan on this page without an account?

No. Scanning and the archive open after you join. There's no uploader and no sample slip on this page.

What is the difference between one slip and a bulk import?

Scanning and saving one slip is free with an account. Importing a stack of slips in one pass is Slots Edge and up.

Does the play logbook replace the W-2G?

No. A jackpot cash-out in the logbook can offer a scan and fill in the date and amount. The form you photograph is still what you check before you save.

Who can see my archive?

Your archive belongs to your account. This page doesn't publish slips, names, or amounts.

Do you keep a public copy of casino W-2Gs?

No. The archive only holds slips you add, and this page shows none of them.
