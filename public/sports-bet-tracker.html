EdgeTilt Sports Bet Tracker is a private log for people who already bet and want the number they took written down. Winning or losing is only half the record. The other half is whether your number beat the close. If your slips are sitting in a camera roll or a notes app right now, this is where they go instead.

It's not a sportsbook. It doesn't take wagers, move lines, or pay out tickets. It's not a password vault either. EdgeTilt never asks for your DraftKings, FanDuel, Circa, or any other sportsbook username or password, and it never signs in to a book as you.

The log is an account tool, and this page is only the intro. You won't see a bet, a unit total, or a closing number here from anyone's account, yours included.

What you get in your account

Once you join, Sports Bet Tracker opens inside EdgeTilt. You can log spreads, totals, moneylines, props, and parlays. Every bet keeps the stake, the price, and the line you actually got. Units and profit stay with the bets you settle. If a bet has a closing number stored, closing line value shows up next to the result. Positive means you beat the close.

You can type a bet in, start one from an odds board in EdgeTilt, or capture a slip from a photo or pasted text. Either way, you confirm every field before it saves, so nothing hits your log on a guess. Some open spread, moneyline, and total bets can settle on their own once the game is final. Player props are yours to grade. And you can always set the result yourself.

How it works

  1. Create an account and open Sports Bet Tracker.
  2. Enter the price, the line you got, and your stake in units or dollars.
  3. If you captured a slip, check it before you save. Fix anything that doesn't match the ticket.
  4. As bets settle, your account keeps profit and units for that log.
  5. Closing line value compares your number to the close on the same side. Not the opener.

Five calculators run on their own pages, in your browser, on the numbers you type: Kelly, no-vig, the odds converter, arbitrage, and CLV. They don't read the tracker, and you don't need an account to use them. The tracker only opens after you join.

FAQ

Is the bet log on this page?

No. This page is just the intro. Your log, your units, and closing line value on the bets you entered open after you create an account and sign in.

Do I give EdgeTilt my sportsbook password?

No. EdgeTilt never asks for sportsbook passwords and never signs in to a book as you. You enter the bet yourself, or you confirm a slip you photographed or pasted.

Are the calculators the same thing as the tracker?

No. Kelly, no-vig, the odds converter, arbitrage, and CLV run in your browser on numbers you type. They don't load your bets. The tracker stays in your account.

What does closing line value mean here?

It's your number versus the close, on the same side. Take a dog at +7 that closes at +3 and you're four points to the good. The public CLV calculator lets you type both numbers yourself. Saved CLV on bets you logged stays in the tracker.

Can anyone see my bets from this page?

No. There's no bet log on this page. The bets you save belong to your account.

Can I use the tracker without joining?

No. The tracker is an account tool. The calculators linked from this page are the ones you can use without logging in.
