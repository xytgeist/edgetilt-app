The tracker is a private log of the bets you enter. Up top you see bankroll, unit size, profit in units, ROI, record, and the share of closes you beat.

ROI is profit in units divided by settled stake in units. Record is wins, losses, and pushes. CLV beat is the share of bets with a closing number where yours was better. A push against the close doesn't count either way. These chips cover the whole log. All, Open, and Settled only filter the list below.

You set the bankroll and unit size. The log opens after you create an account.

How a bet gets in

You add every bet. Nothing comes in from a sportsbook on its own.

  • Type it: event, date, sport, book, market, American odds, and stake. Markets are spread, moneyline, total, player prop, parlay, and other.
  • Long-press a price on a game in EdgeTilt. The form opens with that book and number filled in. You check it, then save.
  • Open the book from a price. That adds an unconfirmed 1 unit bet to the log. Confirm, edit, or delete it.
  • Paste slip text or add up to eight slip photos. Review the fields, then save.
  • Import a CSV. You still confirm before it saves.

Closing line and grades

When EdgeTilt has the closing number for that game, the log shows how many points your spread or total beat it by. That stays in your account and never shows on this page.

After a game ends, EdgeTilt can mark an open spread, moneyline, or total won or lost from the final score, when it matches that game. Parlay legs roll up to the parlay. Player props stay manual. Boxing isn't auto-graded, and UFC and MMA settle the moneyline only. You can override any grade.

What this is not

EdgeTilt never asks for your DraftKings, FanDuel, Circa, or other sportsbook username or password, and never signs in to a book as you. No synced record, no public profile of your bets, no tailing.

That's a demo with made-up bets. Your real log, stakes, and balances stay in your account.

FAQ

Does EdgeTilt sync my sportsbook?

No. You log the bet, paste a slip, add a photo, or import a CSV, and confirm before it saves. EdgeTilt never takes a sportsbook login.

What does the summary show?

Bankroll, unit size, profit in units, ROI, record, and the share of closes you beat. It's one total for the whole log, not a breakdown by sport or book. Sport and book are saved on each bet.

What is CLV beat?

Of your bets with a closing number, the percent where your number was better. Ties with the close don't count.

What settles on its own?

An open spread, moneyline, or total, after the game ends, when EdgeTilt matches that game to the final score. Parlay legs roll up. Props stay manual. You can change any grade.

Do I need an account for the calculators?

No. Kelly, no-vig, the odds converter, arbitrage, and CLV are separate free pages. The tracker opens after you join.
