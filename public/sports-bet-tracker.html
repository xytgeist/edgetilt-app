Sports Bet Tracker

The tracker is a private log of bets you enter. At the top: bankroll, unit size, profit in units, ROI, record, and the share of closes you beat.

ROI is profit in units divided by settled stake in units. The record is wins, losses, and pushes. CLV beat is the share of bets with a closing number where your number was better. A push against the close is left out of that share. These chips cover the whole log. All, Open, and Settled only filter the list under them.

You set the bankroll and the unit size. There is no tracker price on this page. The log opens after you create an account.

How a bet gets in

You put the bet in. Nothing from a sportsbook arrives on its own.

  • Type it: event, date, sport, book, market, American odds, and stake. Markets are spread, moneyline, total, player prop, parlay, and other.
  • Long-press a price on a game in EdgeTilt. The form opens with that book and number filled in. You check it, then save.
  • Open the book from a price. That drops an unconfirmed 1 unit bet on the log. Confirm it, edit it, or delete it.
  • Paste slip text, or add a photo of the slip (up to eight). You review the fields, then save.
  • Import a CSV. You still confirm before it is saved.

Closing line and grades

When EdgeTilt has the closing number for that game, the log shows how many points your spread or total beat it by. That figure stays in your account. It is not on this page.

After a game ends, EdgeTilt can mark an open spread, moneyline, or total won or lost from the final score, when it matches that game. Parlay legs roll up to the parlay. Player props stay manual. You can change a grade. Boxing is not graded for you. UFC and MMA grade the moneyline only.

What this is not

EdgeTilt does not ask for a DraftKings, FanDuel, Circa, or any other sportsbook username or password, and it does not sign in to a book as you. There is no synced record, no public profile of your bets, and no tailing.

This page does not show bets, stakes, or balances.

FAQ

Does EdgeTilt sync my sportsbook?

No. You log the bet, paste a slip, add a photo, or import a CSV. You confirm before it is saved. EdgeTilt does not take a sportsbook login.

What does the summary show?

Bankroll, unit size, profit in units, ROI, record, and the share of closes you beat. It is one total for the log. It is not a breakdown by sport or book. Sport and book are on each bet.

What is CLV beat?

Of the bets that have a closing number, the percent where your number was better. Tied closes are not in that percent.

What settles on its own?

An open spread, moneyline, or total, after the game ends, when EdgeTilt matches that game to the final score. Parlay legs roll up. Props stay manual. You can change a grade.

Do I need an account for the calculators?

No. Kelly, no-vig, the odds converter, arbitrage, and CLV are separate pages. The tracker opens after you join.
