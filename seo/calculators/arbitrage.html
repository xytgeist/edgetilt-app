Arbitrage and hold

Hold is how far the two implied probabilities sit above 100%. If they sit under 100%, the prices arb and a split stake locks a profit either way. Most books you compare on the same market will show a hold, not an arb.

Example: -110 / -110 holds about 4.76% and does not arb. +110 / +110 is an overlay of about 4.76% and locks about $5 on a $100 split.
