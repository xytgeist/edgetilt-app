Arbitrage and hold

Type two American prices and a total to split. If the prices arb, the page splits that total so both sides return the same profit. If they do not, it shows the hold and the fair prices, and it does not show stakes.

EdgeTilt does not place either bet.

The formula

Each American price becomes a decimal. Add 1 / decimal A and 1 / decimal B. That sum is the combined implied probability. Under 100%, the prices arb. Each stake is the total times that side's share of the sum. Locked profit is the smaller payout minus the total.

Hold on this page is (combined implied - 1) times 100. Return is locked profit divided by the total. They are not the same percent with the sign flipped.

Not an arb

-110 and -110, total $100.

Hold 4.76%. Combined implied 104.76%. Fair A -100. Fair B -100. The page does not show stakes. The line is: "No arb. Combined implied is 104.76%. An arb needs that under 100%."

An arb

-105 and +120, total $250.

Hold -3.33%. Combined implied 96.67%. Fair A -113. Fair B +113. Locked profit +$8.60. Return 3.44%. Stake A $132.45. Stake B $117.55.

The line is: "These prices arb. The split locks the same profit either way."

Hold is -3.33% and return is 3.44%. Both figures are right. They measure different things.

Limits

The numbers assume both bets fill at the prices you typed, with no commission and no line move while you are clicking. Sportsbooks may limit accounts or void bets. The split is the two opposite sides of one two-way result. Different settlement rules, or a middle, are a different bet. This page does not send either wager.

FAQ

When is it an arb?

When the combined implied probability is under 100%. At -110/-110 it is 104.76%, so there is no arb and no stake split on the page.

Why don't hold and return match?

Hold is how far the two implied probabilities sit from 100%. Return is locked profit divided by the total. On -105 and +120 those are -3.33% and 3.44%.

Will EdgeTilt place the bets?

No. It only splits the stake you typed.

What if one book voids a side?

Then the other side is a naked bet. The locked profit assumed both bets stood. Books can also limit the account or cut the stake.

Does this read my tracker?

No. Two prices and a total, in the browser. No account.
