Closing line value

Closing line value is your number minus the close, scored so a positive result means you beat the close. It is not the opening line, and it does not say the bet won.

Pick Spread, Total, or Price. Type your number and the closing number. This page does not look up the close.

Use the same side for both line numbers. A favorite at -3 against a close of -6.5 is already the same side. Do not flip one of them.

Line CLV

Spread: your line minus the close.

Total, over: your total minus the close.

Total, under: the close minus your total.

The row is Line CLV, one decimal, then "pts".

Spread

You +7, close +3. Line CLV +4.0 pts. "You beat the close."

You -3, close -6.5. Line CLV +3.5 pts. "You beat the close."

You -3, close -3. Line CLV 0.0 pts. "Same number as the close. That is not a beat."

Total

Under 45, close 48. Line CLV +3.0 pts. "You beat the close."

Over 45, close 48. Line CLV -3.0 pts. "The close beat your number."

Over 47.5, close 46. Line CLV +1.5 pts. "You beat the close."

Price CLV

Price CLV is the closing implied probability minus your price's implied probability, in points. It does not remove the vig from the close first. It is not the spread points stored on a bet in the tracker.

The row is Price CLV, two decimals, then "pts". The hint adds: "Price CLV is probability points, not the spread points stored on a bet."

You +150, close +130. Your price implies 40.00%. The close implies 43.48%. Price CLV +3.48 pts. "You beat the close."

Limits

A better close is a long-run signal. It is not the result of that ticket. Both numbers have to be the same market and the same side. A price gap is not a point gap. The tracker, after you join, can store line CLV on bets it can match. This page never does that lookup.

Runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to EdgeTilt. Saved CLV on your own bets lives in the bet tracker.

FAQ

What does a positive number mean?

You beat the close. It does not mean the bet won.

Do I enter both numbers?

Yes. The page does not look up a close.

Why did the over and the under get opposite signs?

They are opposite bets. Under 45 against a close of 48 is +3.0. Over 45 against that same close is -3.0.

Is price CLV the same as the tracker's CLV?

No. Price CLV is probability points between two American prices. The tracker stores line points on a spread or a total.

Does +150 versus +130 use a devigged close?

No. It is 43.48% minus 40.00%, which is +3.48 points. The vig stays in both prices.
