Closing line value

Closing line value is your number versus the close, not versus the opener. On a spread, CLV is your line minus the closing line on the same side. A dog +7 that closes +3 is +4 points. On a total, an over uses your total minus the close, and an under uses the close minus your total. On a price, CLV is the close's implied probability minus yours, in percentage points. Positive means you beat the close. The bet tracker stores spread and total CLV in points. This page also runs price CLV. Your saved bets and the live close stay in the tracker.

Example: +7 closing at +3 is +4.0 points. Under 45 closing at 48 is +3.0 points. +150 closing at +130 is about +3.48 probability points.
