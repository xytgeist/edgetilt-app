Kelly criterion

Kelly sizes the stake from your edge. It uses the payout on your American odds and the win percent you believe, then multiplies by full, half, or quarter Kelly. Half Kelly is the default because full Kelly swings hard when the percent is off.

Example: a $1,000 bankroll, +150, a 45% win chance, and half Kelly stakes $41.67.
