No-vig fair odds

A two-way price includes the book's hold. Fair odds divide each side's implied probability by the total, which removes that margin. The fair price is the number with no juice.

Example: -110 / -110 is about a 4.76% hold. Fair odds are -100 / -100, a 50% chance each side.
