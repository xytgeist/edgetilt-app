Odds converter

Type one price. The page returns American, decimal, fractional, and the implied percent. American can be +150 or -110. Decimal can be 2.50. Fractional can be 3/2.

Decimal is rounded to the cent. There is no stake field and no payout field.

What the rows mean

American is the US price. Decimal is the total return per 1 unit staked, including stake. Fractional is profit over stake. Implied is the break-even win percent at that price. It still includes the book's hold. Fair odds need both sides, on the no-vig calculator.

FAQ

Which formats can I type?

American (+150, -110), decimal (2.50), or a fraction (3/2, 7/2). One box.

Why is -110 shown as 1.91?

Decimal is rounded to the cent. 1.91 converts back to -110.

Is the implied percent a prediction?

No. It is the break-even percent at that price, hold included.

Where do I remove the vig?

On the no-vig page, with both sides of the bet.
